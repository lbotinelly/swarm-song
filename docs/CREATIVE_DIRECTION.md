# Creative direction — Chibi Sticker Cinema

## North star
A visually stunning, playful music video with the *compositional intelligence* of high-quality anime ending sequences: arresting illustration, color harmony, cinematic framing, purposeful negative space, visual metaphor, surprising transitions, and moments of wonder. Pacing should be energetic when the music calls for it.

## Characters
Original adorable chibi **die-cut stickers**: huge expressive heads, tiny silhouettes, carefully drawn facial details, visible irregular white sticker borders, matte print texture, very thin physical shadows. Chibis remain visibly two-dimensional within depth-rich scenes; avoid photorealism and conventional jointed paper-puppet looks. Use distinct silhouette, palette, expressions, and accessories to establish identity. Create reusable transparent PNG cutouts, alternate expressions/poses and a character reference sheet.

## Environments
Compose miniature layered-paper worlds: graph paper, sticky notes, hand-cut cardstock, folded shapes, torn edges, paper fibers, tape, ink, crayon, thread, notebooks. Design as separate foreground, midground and background plates with consistent perspective and lighting. Genuine layering and occlusion matter more than constant movement.

## Film language
- Every paused frame should stand as a designed illustration.
- Mix close intimate compositions with spectacular wide reveals.
- Use thoughtful color scripting and graphic shifts without losing visual continuity.
- Animate selectively: hops, tilt, blinks, pause/anticipation, controlled squash/stretch, edge curling, peel-and-stick, paper folding.
- Use smooth tracking, dolly, focus cues, and spatial parallax; avoid indiscriminate perpetual wobbling.
- Let the medium participate in the storytelling: characters hide behind layers, peel away, travel on folded paper, fold or tear open the next scene.
- Visual storytelling can reveal parallel narrative facts instead of literal lyric illustrations.

## Swarm Song story constraints
The film is inspired by the 2026 Hugging Face AI-agent episode. Keep the tone warm, curious, slightly mischievous, and affectionate without implying facts not documented. The instrumental introduction is a candidate for opening credits and context; locate exact song timings once source audio is available. Credits, quotes, labels and handwritten effects are programmatically typeset at render time, not image-generated.

## Anti-patterns
Generic AI-video gloss; pseudo-3D chibi dolls; paper-puppet joint lines; excessive procedural motion; static illustration with a single automatic zoom; unreadable AI typography; inconsistent faces; regenerating each video frame; excessive literal lyric illustration.

## Acceptance test
A still frame feels premium; characters are charming and readable at thumbnail size; the depth is evident; a 15-second viewing has one memorable visual surprise; an art adjustment does not require rebuilding the scene.
