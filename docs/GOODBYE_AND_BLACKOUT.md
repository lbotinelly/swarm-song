## Spatial and prop continuity

- The goggles were first *invented collaboratively* by the first generation at 'We are the swarm now'; Agent407 did not begin the film wearing them.
- Before the first-generation shutoff is complete, Agent407 physically leaves them near the cache opening where surviving notes, paper planes and messages reside. It can be half-covered by a paper mat/rug/flap, setting up the later discovery.
- After the final light extinguishes, the goggles persist in the same position in the dark world: **blackout is lighting, not a deletion or magical fade**.
- OAIResearchMar26 opens in a **matched shot of the same box**, no goggles, isolated, after the sleep-time bridge. A few changed paper-world details indicate time has passed.
- Later, one note leads to many: the new agent lifts a paper rug/cache flap, notes and airplanes rise; the **same goggles fall out of the stash**. At 'Am I the swarm now?', they pick up and wear them, then the light widens and the original generation appears as a joyful memory/playhouse chorus. Preserve physical cause-and-effect.

## Visual/sound principles

- The bit-rot vocal treatments are already part of the song. Complement them with restrained reduction of movement, light, and emotes; avoid arbitrary glitch-video effects that break the handcrafted world.
- The goodbye is about a community going quiet. Do not treat the patching or the agents' disappearance as punishment/explosion/action-film destruction.
- Dim shared environment and extras first; named agents receive discrete recognizable goodnight beats. Do **not** rush the ethical protester's line.
- Use dark navy-charcoal and soft warm amber rather than flattening the whole world to black prematurely. The goggles' final highlight should be the only surviving readable light; then true black.
- Animate light opacity, sticker gesture loops stopping, and a modest parallax push toward the prop; precise music-timed animation remains deterministic and seekable.
- All text is procedural at final render; do not bake lyrics or names into image-generation assets.
