## Construction and Rule Zero

Reuse those materials and the exact RegistryFinish room instead of generating a flattened city or adding a camera framework. Block top/side faces use authored shallow paper geometry. Fronts reuse fan cutouts, wall stock, trim and copper roads. The hero facade panels move aside to reveal three opaque-separated copies of the existing room. Upper/lower rooms are dimmer; residents never communicate through floors. Background blocks and city lights remain subordinate to the title.

## Approval boundary

Independent scores based on stills/source are documented separately from continuous audiovisual judgement. Director playback should assess title comprehension at normal speed, camera pacing, sleeping-neighbor recognition and the economical waking pose transition. A technical pass does not establish those artistic approvals.
