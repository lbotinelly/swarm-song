# Agent407 — mini style spec

**Visual authority:** `assets/characters/agent407/concepts/standing-v1.png`. Refine this character; do not redesign it. Ratios below are working measurements from that accepted image, rounded to practical targets—not claims of exact anatomical measurements from a painted drawing. **W = head width across the cheeks, excluding the white border and ears** (about 700 source pixels). Compare poses at equal W, never equal canvas height.

| Feature | Locked working rule |
|---|---|
| Body proportions | Head: **1.00 W wide × 0.67 W high**, excluding ears. Chin-to-soles: **0.51 W**. Torso width: **0.49 W**; relaxed arm-to-arm span: **0.71 W**. Visible standing leg below the crotch: **0.14 W**. Total standing height, ear tip to sole: about **1.63 W**. Keep these within **±5%** for straight-on poses. Foreshortening may shorten projected lengths; kneeling must not lengthen thighs, shins or paws. |
| Outline | Warm dark brown, gently irregular rounded strokes: **0.015–0.020 W** on the outer contour, **0.008–0.014 W** for interior marks. Scale strokes with the sprite, not the output screen. No pure-black vector-clean or uniformly heavy outlines. |
| Watercolor | Warm cream base, pale peach washes, muted rosy/lavender shading, soft paper grain. Low-contrast mottling; retain clear facial marks. No glossy highlights, hard airbrush gradients, or dimensional doll rendering. Match the accepted master's softness. |
| Die-cut border | Warm-white continuous silhouette-following band, typically **0.045–0.060 W**. Rounded joins, slight handmade variation. Do not outline internal details in white or fill the transparent spaces between limbs unnecessarily. |
| Shadow | Keep the existing soft painted color shading **inside** the character. **No baked external glow, ground shadow or directional cast shadow.** Scene renderer supplies contact/drop shadows consistently. |
| Eyes / mouth | Two simple dark-brown vertical ovals, about **0.065 W wide × 0.17 W high**; no sclera, lashes or new shiny pupils. Small centered open smile, about **0.11 W wide**, warm coral interior. Singing opens that mouth modestly; surprise uses a small round “o”; cheer may close eyes to simple arcs. Preserve eye spacing and blush placement. |
| Transparency / export | One pose per native-resolution **lossless RGBA PNG**, genuine alpha, no matte, text, props or emotes. Keep returned originals unchanged. Check on cream and dark navy at native and delivery scale. Flag background speckles, halos or translucent interiors; do not silently threshold or upscale. Record actual dimensions, prompt, reference, version and hash. |
| Padding | For new masters, aim for **at least 6% of canvas width/height on each corresponding edge**, including the white border. Never clip ears/paws. Preserve common pixel density rather than enlarging a crouch to fill its canvas. The accepted standing-v1 has only ~4% vertical clearance: retain it as the style reference; any padded production derivative must be separate and labeled. |

**Pose check:** overlay/compare at equal head width; inspect ear/face identity, chin-to-body size, limb lengths, cut border and light/dark alpha edges. Standing-v1 is the proportion authority; kneeling-v1's extended folded legs are explicitly **not** an anatomy reference. Accessories remain separate. Supporting cast shares this body grammar; only restrained facial, ear, blush, posture or accent differences are allowed.
