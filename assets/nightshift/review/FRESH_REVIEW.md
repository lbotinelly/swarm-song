## Findings and prioritized direction

**Wardrobe:** strengths: recognizable shared anatomy/face/print; roles survive120px; PhaseOne's occupational signal, Courier's blue/gold contrast and Protester's sign silhouette are distinct. Limitations: equipment contours slightly more controlled than baseline; protester outlines feel finer; allowed face overlap reduces expression space. These are compatible equipped copies, not anatomical redesigns. Future pose batches need equal-head-width comparisons to keep drift from accumulating. No extra clothing warranted.

**Props:** strengths: plane silhouette immediately readable in scene; dust belongs to same watercolor language; environmental cloud edges/rim light effective without character-style white border. Limitations: dust contrast on beige initially unproven; major plane orientation changes untested; cloud/internal planes are one composite. Priority: test dust in actual warm scene, keep plane's fixed-view tilt modest, retain cloud as coherent plane.

**Worlds/staging:** strengths: village thread/mailboxes/patches communicate inhabited community; two paths and foreground clearing provide hierarchy; box cardboard and registry placement offer an intimate ungoggled stage. Limitations: support cast looks posed rather than independently alive; neutral box stance does not yet express discovery; scene studies do not establish full action-shot integration. These are performance tasks for a later shot, not reasons to regenerate scenery. Civilization is the strongest world study.

**Dark box:** readable face/location, but gray treatment flattens paper richness and makes the character seem exempt from darkness rather than sharing a clear light source. Priority: revise or omit from approved art; retained here only as a clearly marked diagnostic. This assessment is about lighting quality, not failure to depict literal blackout.

**Sky:** strengths: scale/negative space, clear diagonal journey, unobstructed final visual release. Foreground contour changes relative to stars; no obvious rectangular extraction edges or exposed boundaries in samples. Limitations: four stills cannot establish smooth travel/easing, all intermediate occlusion, or final-hold pacing. Priority: human MP4 playback review. No additional illustration warranted from these stills.
