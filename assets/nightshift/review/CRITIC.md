Critic selected sky for lean parallax despite civilization's stronger location score.

**Sky v2:** strengths: unwanted content removed, glow retained, corridor preserved. Limitations: slightly embossed texture versus v1, busy upper stars, baked distant clouds. Retain; fly below star cluster and use separate foreground bank.

**Plane:** strengths: crisp small-scale silhouette, palette match, fold/white contour. Limitations: strong dark fold, fixed view, no standalone message cue. Retain; modest tilt only, narrative meaning comes from staging.

**Dust:** strengths: directional growing lobes, matching watercolor/border, small-scale readability. Limitations: low contrast on bright cream, cloud-like when far from feet, obvious identical repeats. Retain; short lives near feet and small scale/offset variations in future running shot.

**Cloud:** strengths: tactile cut layers, warm rim, useful alpha silhouette. Limitations: straight bottom/right edges must stay offscreen, repeated contours if duplicated, internal shadows baked as one coherent bank. Retain as one plane with bounded travel.

**Initial staging:** civilization had strong singer hierarchy, costume identity and path separation, but courier floated above path, all feet lacked sufficient contact, and bright clean borders stood apart from worn scenery. Prioritized foot placement and compact soft shadows; no regeneration. Dark box preserved location/face/canon but uniform dimming flattened material hierarchy, muddied gray subjects, and retained daytime light relationships. Prioritized localized warm pool/cool corners or honest diagnostic label.

**Initial sky sequence:** strengths: clear plane, actual alpha occlusion at150, differential cloud/star motion.
