# Childlike authorship, careful animation

- **Hills:** awkward scissors-cut silhouettes, exposed fibers, patchy green wax crayon, yellow/blue stray marks and uncovered paper. Each hill and all its marks are one rigid image. No rubber deformation or animated texture.
- **Houses:** small independent front-facing assemblies. Crooked roofs, mismatched windows, clumsy crayon decoration and visible tape. Attached details move with the house. Doors stay closed in this study, so no artificial hinge animation is implied.
- **Sky:** warm paper stock with a few authored blue and ochre crayon-like strokes. The path draws on the backing; stray marks on the backing remain there. The sun is an independent imperfect yellow disk with connected rays.
- **Tissue fog:** the user's added direction permits visually sophisticated material choices. One translucent sheet, using the existing paper stock at 16% opacity with a fixed soft alpha edge with a pale grade, lies in front of the distant hill and behind the houses. Its shape and fibers move together. This material treatment is part of the village design approved by Leo on November 13, 2026; it is not volumetric fog or a particle effect.
- **Layering:** backing → sun → distant hill → tissue → cloud → house/smoke assemblies → bunny interaction → foreground hill. The physical GPU inset remains separate. Shadows are shallow paper-on-paper contact cues, not cinematic 3D lighting.
- **Smoke:** a single flat S-shaped paper strip with a long lower stem. It rises through a hidden rectangular channel ending at the chimney lip. The strip is never stretched; the lower end remains concealed. No detached particle puffs.
- **Characters:** exact shared approved cream bunny model. Existing organizer/courier wardrobe derivatives are reused as staging proposals. Anchor-based pose swaps, small foot-pivot tilts and restrained hops preserve anatomy.
- **Camera:** front-facing 2D translations only. Far/middle/near layers travel 12/32/65 pixels over the final camera move. No large perspective turns. Negative space leaves the sun, smoke and social interaction readable.
- **Text:** any visible wording is runtime typography. The inset's small caption clarifies imagined connection; notes remain blank.

The GPU world remains constrained and architectural. The village is colorful and intuitive. The shared craft identity links them; no literal escape from physical compartments is depicted.
