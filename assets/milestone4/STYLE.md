# GPU apartments — M4 proposal

User's latest construction direction: one 2.5D GPU slab, apartments stacked INSIDE it, all verandas on the SAME narrow edge, facing the SAME outward direction. Continuous floor/ceiling slabs block vertical sightlines. Keep residents recessed; never stage occupied towers facing one another. Audience sees the fan face and inhabited edge from a shallow three-quarter angle. V1/v2 staggered facade concepts are superseded.

Materials: watercolor teal cardstock housing, cream/kraft folded fans, corrugated fins and bracket, ochre connector teeth. Tiny lamps, curtains, bedding, shelves and repairs distinguish homes. No glossy electronics, neon or baked words. Character white cut borders stay distinct from dimensional paper scenery.

World scale: room openings accommodate the approved bunny head/body; exterior establishing shots carry lights and architecture, close interiors carry performance. Production will need separately illustrated front rails for exterior acting; these animatics keep actors inside room shots.

Lighting: lavender twilight/amber homes → individual farewell pools → goggles alone → literal black → changing paper daylight → warm quiet morning. Background detail must reach zero before the goggles. No UI/text appears during the blackout.

Second apartment v2 is a different layout: centrally visible ochre bed, blue curtain, small shifted lamp, floating shelf/cup; no cabinet/plant/cache/notes/goggles. The awakening remains before inheritance. This latest M4 user instruction overrides older documents' same-box location for this proposal. The shared baseline body remains unchanged.

The closed-eye expression is one separately versioned animatic study, not approval of a new character or pose inventory. Existing standing pose returns on waking. Pillow/quilt occlusion and a small whole-sticker rise/tilt provide economical acting; no ear rig or anatomical puppet.

All new scenery and expression studies await human art direction. Internal review is not canon approval.
