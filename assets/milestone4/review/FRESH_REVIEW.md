## A — strongest emotional proposal, mechanism still distracting

The worn stitched book makes the architecture feel remembered and connects naturally to the hidden notes. Resting spreads are the best transformation compositions. However, frames595/610/655/680/725/750 show a large straight pale sheet dominating the image, reading as a board passing in front of the book rather than a leaf belonging to its uneven stack. Tiny flattened tower images contrast awkwardly with upright towers. Spreads repeatedly return to the same arrangement: page turns read more strongly than distinct eras, weather or aging. The persistent landmark works; meaningful historical change remains weak.

## B — clearest spatial treatment and economical fallback

Near/far scale, distant rows and the open corridor communicate travel without another metaphor. But identical cutouts and broad empty center feel like an architectural display. Flat bases and fixed illustrated perspective do not fully root buildings in moving terrain. The original city concept has richer infrastructure/occlusion/atmosphere. Day/night changes alone do not establish historical distance. Keep B as fallback if A's mechanism distracts in playback.

## C — legible fold study, least moving

Panels remain visibly joined and the continuous route clarifies the map. The finite construction is sensible. Yet three evenly spaced duplicate towers, pale rectangles and smooth stripe feel diagrammatic. Frames610/635/655 resemble compressed buildings on a folding screen. Large empty upper space and the return to three duplicates communicate reversal of an effect more than emergence of a new neighborhood. Retain as documented exploration rather than adding complexity.

## Shared sequence findings

- **Architecture:** v3 convincingly reads as a GPU slab containing stacked edge rooms. Same-facing verandas and slabs support isolation; fans, corrugation, repairs and domestic details are strong. Empty exterior concepts do not independently verify every resident sightline.
- **Goodbye:** cozy rooms, but cheerful frontal poses feel more like introductions than farewells. Agent407's cache action is most purposeful; repeated/mirrored bedroom details weaken individual-home identity.
- **Goggles/black:** frames375/435/465/510 establish cache, isolated goggles, dim remainder and black. Strong sampled continuity. Technical QA separately verifies all encoded blackout frames; reviewer did not independently inspect every frame or felt duration.
- **Awakening:** changed bed/curtain/shelves and absent cache identify a different residence. Ungoggled bunny protected by quilt supports vulnerability. Final smile is warm rather than tentative, but not triumphant.
- **Matched composition:** broad rhyme exists, but bed foreground, scale and camera differ. This is a composition reference, not a finished matched opening.
- **Destination continuity:** the approach frame800 shows pink curtains, blue-check bedding and a plant; the new interior has blue curtains/ochre bedding. Without a clearly identified destination, it risks implying the wrong room. This remains an unresolved animatic limitation affecting all three.
